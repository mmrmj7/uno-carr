# Web Development & AI Storytelling Video Agency Website

A modern, high-converting website built for an agency that delivers custom websites, business automation tools, and AI promotional video advertisements.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Included

1. **Brand & Logo Placeholder Ready**
   - Configured cleanly with `[YOUR BRAND NAME]` and `[BRAND LOGO]` placeholders.
   - Easily update your brand name, phone, email, and UPI ID centrally in `app.js` (`BRAND_CONFIG`).

2. **Light Theme (Preferred Default) + Dark Mode Toggle**
   - Clean, trustworthy aesthetic with light mode by default.
   - 1-click sun/moon theme switcher button that persists user selection.

3. **Solar & Vendor Business Automation Showcase (Live Interactive Demo)**
   - **Quotation Generator**: Interactive calculator for solar capacity, base equipment costs, govt subsidy rebates, GST, and net payable calculations with WhatsApp & PDF download simulation.
   - **Work Completion Certificate**: Digital sign-off report with checklist verification (eliminating MS Word/Excel paper delays).
   - **Digital Letterhead Generator**: Live printable/PDF letterhead ready for instant editing and official correspondence.

4. **AI Storytelling & Promotional Video Studio Section**
   - Dedicated showcase of AI video promotional ads (problem-solution narrative hooks, voiceovers, dynamic sound design, Reels/Shorts format).
   - Video player modal and direct booking CTAs for video ads.

5. **Live Portfolio Showcase with Site Dialogs**
   - Large browser mockup preview cards.
   - Click to open the live site dialog with editable URL targets and descriptions.

6. **4 Pricing Plans (Fully Customizable)**
   - **Basic Starter** (₹4,999)
   - **Growth Business** (₹9,999)
   - **Pro Business Suite** (₹16,999) — *Recommended & Most Popular*
   - **Enterprise AI Automation** (₹29,999)

7. **Direct Frictionless Booking & Checkout (No Admin/Login Hassles)**
   - Clean booking modal with client contact inputs and project requirements.
   - **Coupon Engine**:
     - `VIP50` — 50% VIP discount
     - `DISCOUNT10` — 10% discount
     - `EARLYBIRD` — ₹2,000 flat savings
     - `SOLAR25` — 25% vendor partner discount
   - **PhonePe & UPI Payment Gateway Simulation**:
     - PhonePe branding, UPI QR preview, UPI ID copy, and instant payment validation.
   - **Instant Email Confirmation & Notification Receipt**:
     - Displays order ID, email confirmation dispatch notification, and a prompt letting the client know your team will follow up within 2 hours.

## 🚀 How to Run & Customize

### Quick Preview:
Simply double-click or open `index.html` in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

### Updating Your Brand Name and Logo:
Open `app.js` and edit the configuration at the top:
```javascript
const BRAND_CONFIG = {
  brandName: "Your Brand Name Here",
  brandTagline: "Web Development & AI Storytelling Automation",
  contactPhone: "+91 98765 43210",
  contactEmail: "hello@yourbrand.com",
  phonePeUpiId: "yourupi@ybl",
};
```
