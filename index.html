Questions before booking?

Some common questions about an UNO Car booking.

How can I book a one way cab?

Enter your pickup and drop location in the booking form. Your enquiry will open in WhatsApp for confirmation.

Will I know the fare before booking?

UNO Car can confirm the applicable fare and trip details before you finalize the booking.

Can I book an airport transfer?

Yes. Select the Airport option and send your pickup and drop details to the UNO Car team.

Which cars can I request?

Sedan, SUV/Ertiga and Innova Crysta categories can be requested subject to availability.

Can hotels and travel agents contact UNO Car?

Yes. Hotels and travel agents can contact the team regarding regular guest transfers and B2B requirements.

Are tolls and parking included?

This can vary by route and booking. Confirm toll, parking, taxes and driver charges with UNO Car before the trip.
